\begin{table*}[t]
\caption{Representative Linguistic Errors in Sentiment and Emotion Predictions}
\label{tab:error-analysis}
\centering
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.13\textwidth}X p{0.16\textwidth} p{0.27\textwidth}}
\toprule
Error Type & Roman Urdu Tweet with English Translation & Labels and Prediction & Linguistic Mechanism \& Description \\
\midrule
\textbf{Sarcasm} & \emph{Petrol 300 rupees fe litre karne par hukumat ka dil se shukriya.} (Heartfelt thanks to the government for making petrol 300 rupees per liter.) & Truth: Joy; sentiment: Positive; predicted: Negative (SVM). & Sarcasm expresses negative frustration using the word ``shukriya'' (thanks) after a price increase, so surface-positive wording conflicts with intent. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Negation Scope} & \emph{Mein aap ki policies se bilkul mutafiq nahi hoon, awam tang aa chuki hai.} (I do not agree with your policies at all; the public is fed up.) & Truth: Sad; sentiment: Negative; predicted: Positive (NB). & The negation marker ``nahi'' (not) reverses ``mutafiq'' (agree). Bag-of-words models can miss this scope relation. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Code-Mixing} & \emph{ye class bohot bad thi, bilkul maza nahi aya.} (This class was very bad; did not enjoy it at all.) & Truth: Sad; sentiment: Negative; predicted: Positive (NB). & Roman Urdu and English are mixed in one short tweet. Sparse lexical models are sensitive to spelling variation and context loss. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Ambiguous Weak Label} & \emph{Ya Allah hamare haal par rehm farma aur azab se bacha le.} (O Allah, have mercy on our state and save us from punishment.) & Truth: Joy; sentiment: Positive; predicted: Sad (CNN). & The prayer contains fear and plea semantics but is weakly labeled as Joy, likely because of positive or devotional cues in the labeling heuristic. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}
\end{table*}
